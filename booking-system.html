Admin booking and venue management

Control the whole venue from one place.

The administration area gives the team practical control of restaurant settings, tables, services and every reservation throughout the day.

Staff can create telephone or walk-in reservations, view genuine availability, select an available table when needed, update customer details, change booking status and manage the venue without using a separate paper diary. Admin bookings use the same availability protection as online bookings, helping prevent overlapping table assignments.

Why a connected restaurant platform?
